How this estimate works

Forward prices, volatility and settlement ranges are illustrative. The annual range uses a seeded simulation of monthly average prices; each month is sampled independently. Protection payouts are deducted from the commodity bill and the upfront premium is included. The range is not a confidence guarantee or a forecast.
