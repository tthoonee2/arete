QUESTIONS
Common questions.

    
Is this insurance?
It works like insurance but it’s a cash-settled option on a public market price. You pay once. If the monthly average crosses your limit, we pay the difference.

    
Which price do you use?
The monthly average of the benchmark shown on each product, such as ICE Brent or LME copper.

    
My supplier’s price isn’t exactly the benchmark.
Most local prices move with a benchmark. Regional spread products cover the gap, for example Italian gas over Dutch TTF.

    
When do I get paid?
After each covered month ends and the average is known.

    
Can I end up owing money?
Not with a fixed price. With “Nothing today” you pay the difference if prices move the other way.
