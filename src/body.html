TRUST
Built to institutional standards.
How Arête is designed to operate: execution, risk, client money and security.

  
Prototype — this page describes the intended operating model, not current authorisations.

  

    

      
EXECUTION
Licensed counterparty
Protections are issued through a regulated partner. Arête never takes a directional view on client positions.

      
RISK
Hedged back-to-back
Every client position is offset on ICE or LME, netted across the book, within daily limits per market.

      
SETTLEMENT
Official benchmarks only
Payouts use published exchange settlements and assessments, never an in-house price.

      
CLIENT MONEY
Segregated
Premiums and payouts flow through segregated client accounts, reconciled daily.

      
SECURITY
SSO, 2FA, audit trail
Role-based access, SAML single sign-on and an immutable audit log on every action.

      
DATA
Swiss and EU hosting
Client data stays in Switzerland or the EU, encrypted at rest and in transit.

QUESTIONS
Common questions.

    
Is this insurance?
It works like insurance but it’s a cash-settled option on a public market price. You pay once. If the monthly average crosses your limit, we pay the difference.

    
Which price do you use?
The monthly average of the benchmark shown on each product, such as ICE Brent or LME copper.

    
My supplier’s price isn’t exactly the benchmark.
Most local prices move with a benchmark. Regional spread products cover the gap, for example Italian gas over Dutch TTF.

    
When do I get paid?
After each covered month ends and the average is known.

    
Can I end up owing money?
Not with a fixed price. With “Nothing today” you pay the difference if prices move the other way.
