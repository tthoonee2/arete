1. **Waitlist launch.** Replace "Get protected" with "Request access"; capture company, commodity, volume, country.
2. **Real terrain.** Run `tools/terrain/build_real_terrain.py` on swisstopo data.
3. **Pricing service.** Move the engine server-side; live curves and vol surfaces from licensed data.
4. **Partner and licensing.** Regulated counterparty, appropriateness checks, EMIR reporting.
5. **Onboarding.** KYC/KYB, credit limits for collars, signed confirmations.
6. **Settlement.** Monthly statements, payouts, reconciliation.
7. **Basis lab on real history.** Benchmark history from licensed data; invoice upload.
8. **Distribution.** Widget and API for banks, fuel cards, ERPs.
